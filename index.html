Y7 Biology practical: cell structures and organelles

Explore a cell, make an enzyme and release it · Made by Dr Daniel Mompel Riera

As long as you need: it saves as you go. About 60 to 90 minutes.

Shrinking you to the size of a protein…

Your mission

Make amylase and release it from the cell.

Amylase is the enzyme in your saliva that digests starch.

How the game works

  • 🔬You are inside one cell of a salivary gland: the cell that makes saliva.
  • 📍Each place in the cell does one job. Press a glowing place to go there.
  • 💤A place wakes when you carry what it needs. You choose the order of some places.
  • ✅After each place, answer its checks. Stuck? Use a hint.
  • ✦Jobs done + every check right = ✦ done, like a level in a game.
  • 🔊Hard words: press UK or US to hear them. Music: press 🔈 at the top.
