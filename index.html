How the game works

For pupils

You are inside one cell of a salivary gland. Your mission: make the enzyme amylase and release it from the cell.

In this game, each place of the cell does one job. A place wakes when you carry what it needs, so you can choose the order of some places. Every step waits for you.

After each place, check what you learned: a first letter, the word list or a hint is there if you are stuck. A place is done (✦) when you have done its jobs and every check is right, like finishing a level of a game: then what it gave you wakes the next places.

🔈 Sound, at the top left: music for your journey from the moment you dive in, by the film composer Scott Buckley, and soft sounds when a place wakes or you collect something.

Hard words have UK and US buttons: press one to hear how to say the word. They are under the word's meaning, in the word bank and under each place's definition. The recordings are from the Cambridge Dictionary. The music pauses while a word plays.

For the teacher

A pupil signed in with their school Google account has every check and every finished place saved for you, live on the teacher page's 🔬 Practicals board. Signed out, the game works the same and nothing leaves the computer.

The world shows the true shapes and places of the parts of a salivary gland cell; the colours and the light are made magical, and molecules are drawn much larger than they are.

"See the real thing" shows real diagrams, electron micrographs and a real cell in 3D.
